# AirAware AI — Demo Video Recording Script

**Target Duration:** 3 to 4 Minutes  
**Tone:** Confident, clear, professional, evidence-focused  

---

### Sequence 1: Opening & Problem Statement (0:00 - 0:30)
- **Visual:** Open `AirAware AI` in the browser at the Overview tab.
- **Voiceover:** 
  > "Welcome to AirAware AI. Most air quality apps are passive dashboards—they show a number like AQI 180, but fail to tell families what practical decisions they should make today. AirAware AI is an India-first environmental intelligence assistant that transforms live telemetry and atmospheric models into personalized, low-emission daily routines."

---

### Sequence 2: Scientific Honesty & Data Source Transparency (0:30 - 1:00)
- **Visual:** Point to the AQI card header: highlight the badge **"Modelled Atmospheric Estimate (CAMS / Open-Meteo)"** and the **"Indicative Derived Index"** label. Click location selector and switch between Mumbai and Delhi.
- **Voiceover:**
  > "Notice how AirAware AI maintains strict scientific integrity. Rather than pretending regional atmospheric models are rooftop sensors, we transparently classify Open-Meteo data as a Modelled Atmospheric Estimate from the Copernicus CAMS service. We show the 11-kilometer grid cell resolution and explain that derived indices apply official CPCB NAQI breakpoints for indicative comparison."

---

### Sequence 3: Household Personalization ("My Family") (1:00 - 1:30)
- **Visual:** Click "My Family" button in the header. Edit household size, toggle "Children in Home", "Senior Citizens", and select "Two-Wheeler Commute". Click Save.
- **Voiceover:**
  > "Recommendations adapt to the specific vulnerability of the household. A toddler breathes up to 50% more air per kg than an adult, so their outdoor play is rescheduled away from morning inversion hours. Seniors receive warnings on cardiovascular strain, and two-wheeler commuters receive specific N95 respirator advice against direct tailpipe plumes."

---

### Sequence 4: 24-Hour Time-Based Exposure Planner (1:30 - 2:00)
- **Visual:** Navigate to "Daily Planner" tab. Scroll through Morning, Afternoon, Evening, and Night windows.
- **Voiceover:**
  > "Air pollution changes throughout the day. Our 24-Hour Planner reflects boundary layer physics: in the afternoon, solar radiation disperses ground particulates, making it the safest window for cross-ventilation, whereas morning rush-hour traps exhaust at breathing height."

---

### Sequence 5: Hybrid GenAI Assistant in Action (2:00 - 2:30)
- **Visual:** Click "Ask AI" in the header to open the drawer. Click the prompt chip: *"Is it safe for my children to play outside today?"*.
- **Voiceover:**
  > "AirAware AI answers instantly through a deterministic CPCB engine. It needs no GPU, no model download and no API key, and it explicitly knows whether data is modelled or observed, ensuring zero hallucinations."

---

### Sequence 6: Indoor Air Doctor (2:30 - 3:00)
- **Visual:** Open "Indoor Doctor" tab. Select "Mosquito coils burned at night" and "Wall Exhaust Fan", then click "Generate Indoor Risk Audit".
- **Voiceover:**
  > "Most apps fake indoor AQI. AirAware AI takes an honest stance: our Indoor Air Doctor is a hazard assessment tool. Burning a single mosquito coil emits fine particulate matter equal to 75–100 cigarettes. We provide immediate zero-cost solutions and bust myths, clarifying why potted plants cannot replace certified HEPA filtration."

---

### Sequence 7: Cleaner Choices & What-If Calculator (3:00 - 3:30)
- **Visual:** Navigate to "Cleaner Choices", highlight the high-AQI safety warning (refusing to recommend cycling in toxic smog). Then open "What-If Impact" and adjust the commute slider to 25 km.
- **Voiceover:**
  > "Our Cleaner Choices planner gates outdoor physical activity by current AQI. In the What-If Calculator, commuters can see avoided CO2e and fuel cost savings using documented ARAI emission factors, keeping greenhouse gas mitigation clearly distinguished from personal exposure protection."

---

### Sequence 8: Evaluator Demonstration Scenarios (3:30 - 4:00)
- **Visual:** Click "Demo Scenarios" in the header. Activate "Scenario 4: Missing Monitoring Station (Remote/Rural)" to show transparent lack of fabricated data, then activate "Scenario 5: API Outage" to show stale caching and graceful degradation.
- **Voiceover:**
  > "For evaluators, our built-in Scenario Switcher allows testing all 6 mandatory edge cases in one click: from Mumbai families to missing stations and API outages. AirAware AI is competition-ready, mathematically grounded, and built to make every breath safer."
